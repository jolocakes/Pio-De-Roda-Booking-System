Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who this policy covers: this policy applies to personal information collected through the Pio De Roda Cafe and Restaurant booking website and to the reservation records we keep when you book by phone, message, or in person.

Pio De Roda Cafe and Restaurant ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this booking website, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights over it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The personal information controller is Pio De Roda Cafe and Restaurant, located at 508 De Ocampo St., Indang, Cavite 4122, Philippines. You can reach our data protection contact at pioderodabooking@gmail.com or (046) 415 0390 / 0926 684 5833.

2. Information we collect

  • Booking details: name, contact person, phone number, email address, event address, event date and time, event type, guest count, and chosen services or packages.
  • Payment information: the amount paid, payment method, and reference numbers. We do not store full card numbers; card and e-wallet payments are handled by our payment provider, Xendit Philippines, Inc.
  • Messages: the content of inquiries you send through the contact form.
  • Reviews: the rating and feedback you submit after a completed event.
  • Technical data: a hashed form of your IP address used for security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. We do not keep the raw address in those logs.

3. Why we use your information

  • To review, confirm, and manage your reservation.
  • To contact you about your booking, payment, or changes to your event.
  • To prepare the services you ordered.
  • To keep records required for accounting and legal compliance.
  • To protect the website from spam, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • To improve our services using aggregated, non-identifying reports.

4. Legal basis

We process your information to perform the contract you enter into when you book with us, to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in running and securing our business. Where consent is required, we ask for it separately and you may withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with service providers who help us operate, such as our email provider, payment processor, and hosting provider. These providers are required to protect your data and may not use it for their own marketing. We do not sell your personal information.

Current providers: Google (Gmail) for booking email, Xendit Philippines, Inc. for online payments, and our contracted website hosting provider for the booking system and database.

RecordRetention period

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is processed;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • object to or withdraw consent for certain processing;
  • request deletion or blocking of your data where the law allows;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, email pioderodabooking@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by law.

8. Cookies and similar technology

This site uses only the storage needed to keep you signed in (admin area) and to remember your verified booking on the current tab. If we add analytics or advertising cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed credentials, access controls, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be announced on this page.
