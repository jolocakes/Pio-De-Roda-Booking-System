Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

How this policy works: the self-service windows below match what the booking system enforces. If you need a change outside those windows, contact us and we will review it case by case.

1. Self-service changes

Using the Booking Status page and your Reservation ID + Booking PIN, you can:

  • Cancel up to 7 days before the event date.
  • Reschedule up to 3 days before the event date, once per booking.

Closer to the event, or for any other change, contact us directly at (046) 415 0390 / 0926 684 5833. We will do our best, but changes may not be possible once preparations have started.

2. Cancellation by the customer

When you cancelWhat happens to your payment
More than 30 days before the event 100% refund of the amount paid, less any non-refundable payment-processing or third-party fees already incurred.
15–30 days before the event 50% refund of the amount paid.
8–14 days before the event 25% refund of the amount paid.
7 days or fewer before the event No refund. Please contact us — we may offer a reschedule instead.

3. Rescheduling

One reschedule is allowed per booking when requested at least 3 days before the event, subject to the new date being available. Any price difference between the old and new date or package is payable by the customer. Additional reschedules are at our discretion and may incur a fee of PHP 500.

4. Cancellation by us

If we cannot deliver your confirmed booking because of circumstances within our control, we will tell you as soon as possible and offer either a full refund of amounts paid or a reschedule to a mutually agreeable date. If a force majeure event (severe weather, government restrictions, or a similar event outside our control) makes the event impossible, the same options apply.

5. No-shows

If you do not arrive and cannot be reached on the event date, the booking is treated as a cancellation within 7 days. The deposit is forfeited and no refund is issued.

6. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible, within 14 business days after approval. Bank or e-wallet processing times may add a few days. Refunds are made in Philippine pesos.

7. Non-refundable items

  • Custom items already ordered or produced (printing, personalized giveaways, special ingredients).
  • Third-party fees already paid on your behalf.
  • Deposits already forwarded to third-party suppliers for your event.

8. How to request

Cancel or reschedule from the Booking Status page, or contact us at pioderodabooking@gmail.com / (046) 415 0390 / 0926 684 5833. Include your Reservation ID so we can find your booking quickly.
