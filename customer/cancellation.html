One reschedule is allowed per booking when requested at least 3 days before the event, subject to the new date being available. 4. Cancellation by us

If we cannot deliver your confirmed booking because of circumstances within our control, we will tell you as soon as possible and offer either a full refund of amounts paid or a reschedule to a mutually agreeable date. If a force majeure event (severe weather, government restrictions, or a similar event outside our control) makes the event impossible, the same options apply.
